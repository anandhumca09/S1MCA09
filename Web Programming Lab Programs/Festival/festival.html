Top 3 Festival in India

Diwali

Diwali comes from the Sanskrit word "Deepavali," which means a row of lamps.It is one of the most popular and important festivals in India.People of many faiths, including Hindus, Sikhs, and Jains, celebrate it.It falls every year between mid-October and mid-November on a moonless night called Amavasya.




Onam

Celebrated over 10 days, beginning on the day of Atham and ending on Thiruvonam, the most important day.Marks a time of harvest, prosperity, and the symbolic homecoming of the benevolent Asura (demon) King Mahabali.Observed by Malayalis globally regardless of caste, creed, or religion.




Holi

Known as the Festival of Colors, it marks the arrival of spring and the end of winter.It is a two-day festival.It celebrates the triumph of good over evil and commemorates the divine love between Radha and Krishna.
