Diwali

Diwali is the grand festival of lights that symbolizes the victory of good over evil and light over darkness.

What is Diwali?

  • Diwali comes from the Sanskrit word "Deepavali," which means a row of lamps.
  • It is one of the most popular and important festivals in India.
  • People of many faiths, including Hindus, Sikhs, and Jains, celebrate it.
  • It falls every year between mid-October and mid-November on a moonless night called Amavasya.

Why Do We Celebrate It?

  • It honors the return of Lord Rama to Ayodhya after 14 years in exile and his victory over the demon king Ravana.
  • Families pray to Goddess Lakshmi and Lord Ganesha on the main night to ask for wealth, wisdom, and prosperity.
  • It reminds everyone that truth and goodness always win in the end.
