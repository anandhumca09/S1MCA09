Holi

Holi is a major Hindu festival of colours, love, and spring celebrated in India and around the world.

The Two Days of Celebration

Day 1: Holika Dahan (Choti Holi)

  • The Ritual: People gather on the eve of Holi to light large bonfires (Holika Dahan).
  • The Legend: Commemorates the burning of the demoness Holika and the protection of the devoted young boy Prahlad by Lord Vishnu.
  • Significance: Symbolizes the burning away of evil spirits, negativity, and bad energy.

Day 2: Rangwali Holi (Dhulandi)

  • Playing with Colours: People take to the streets to smear bright colored powders (gulal) and splash colored water on each other.li>
  • Activities: Children use water guns (pichkaris) and water balloons, accompanied by traditional music, drumming, and dancing.
  • Food & Treats: Families share homemade sweets and drinks like gujiya (sweet fried dumplings) and thandai.
