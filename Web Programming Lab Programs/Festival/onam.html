Onam

Onam is the official state and harvest festival of Kerala, India, celebrated annually during the Malayalam month of Chingam, which overlaps with August and September

The Legend of King Mahabali

  • King Mahabali ruled Kerala with great fairness, love, and wisdom, creating a golden age where there was no poverty, crime, or sorrow.
  • Worried by his growing popularity, the gods sought help from Lord Vishnu, who appeared on earth in his dwarf incarnation, Vamana.
  • Vamana asked Mahabali for a small piece of land, which the generous king readily granted.
  • Vamana then grew so huge that he covered the earth and the heavens in two steps, and Mahabali offered his own head for the third step.
  • Before pushing Mahabali down to the netherworld (Patala), Lord Vishnu granted him a final wish: to return to Kerala once every year to visit his beloved people.

Key Traditions and Celebrations

  • Pookkalam: Intricate and colorful floral carpets laid out in front of homes to welcome King Mahabali.
  • Onasadya: A massive, traditional vegetarian feast containing more than 20 to 30 dishes served fresh on a plantain (banana) leaf.
  • Pulikali: A colorful tiger dance where performers paint their bodies in bright yellow, red, and black to resemble tigers and hunters.
  • Attire: People wear new traditional clothes, specifically the Kasavu saree or Mundu.
